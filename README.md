# MBTA Service Reliability

A better public view of MBTA subway service reliability than [MBTA's own page](https://www.mbta.com/performance-metrics/service-reliability) gives you.

## Why

MBTA's current reliability page only covers the Blue, Orange, and Red lines, says nothing about the Green Line or Mattapan, and says nothing about *how often a line isn't even running its normal service* (construction closures, diversions, shuttle-bus replacements). If you've ever wondered "is the Orange Line down again," their page won't tell you.

## What it does

- **Service availability** — what fraction of the year each line is running its normal, full schedule vs. a reduced/construction pattern, including the actual dates of each closure.
- **Delay / typical trip time** — how late trains actually run, and a guesstimate of real trip time (not just a schedule delta).
- Covers all subway lines, not just the three MBTA's own page tracks.
- Updates daily, automatically, from MBTA's own published performance data.
